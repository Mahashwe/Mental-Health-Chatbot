Overview
The Virtual Therapist Bot is an AI-powered chatbot designed to provide emotional support, self-improvement guidance, and behavioral therapy strategies. It helps users navigate emotions, build resilience, and develop a positive mindset through empathy, exercises, and motivational support.

Features
🧠 Emotional Understanding – Listens actively and responds with empathy.
🎯 Self-Improvement Exercises – Suggests activities for self-awareness and personal growth.
💬 Motivational Support – Provides words of affirmation and encouragement.
🚫 Safe & Non-Diagnostic – Does not diagnose or provide medical advice.
⚠️ Crisis Awareness – Encourages professional help for distress or self-harm concerns.
Usage Instructions
Interact with the bot by sharing your thoughts or feelings.
Receive personalized responses with emotional support and guidance.
Follow suggested exercises to enhance mental well-being.
If in distress, seek professional help as the bot is not a substitute for therapy.
Constraints & Safety
❌ The bot does not diagnose mental health conditions.
❌ It avoids misleading or harmful responses and profanity.
⚠️ If self-harm or suicidal thoughts are expressed, the bot directs users to seek immediate help.
